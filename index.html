Flash Timeline

Golden Age

Jay Garrick - The Original Flash

1940

Jay Garrick gains super speed from inhaling heavy water vapors, becoming the first Flash and founding member of the Justice Society.

Silver Age

Barry Allen - The Scarlet Speedster

1956

Police scientist Barry Allen is struck by lightning and doused with chemicals, gaining incredible speed and becoming the Flash of the Silver Age.

Crisis

Crisis on Infinite Earths

1985

Barry Allen makes the ultimate sacrifice, running into the Anti-Monitor's cannon to save the multiverse, seemingly dying in the process.

Modern Era

Wally West - The Fastest Man Alive

1986

Barry's nephew Wally West, formerly Kid Flash, takes up the mantle and eventually surpasses Barry's speed records.

Rebirth

Barry's Return

2008

Barry Allen returns from the Speed Force in Final Crisis, reclaiming his role as The Flash and continuing his heroic legacy.

Speed Force Legacy

The Speed Force connects all speedsters across time and space, creating an eternal legacy of heroes who run faster than thought itself. From Jay Garrick's golden beginnings to Barry Allen's ultimate sacrifice and triumphant return, the Flash represents hope, heroism, and the power to outrun any darkness.
